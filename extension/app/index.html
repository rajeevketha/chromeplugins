Privacy & data access
  • Uses your open Salesforce tab session in the browser only — OrgKit does not upload your sid.
  • What you can query or edit is whatever Salesforce allows for your user (profile, permission sets, object/field access).
  • Deletes and saves ask for confirmation before changing org data.

Privacy policy ·

Works with standard objects, custom objects (__c), and custom metadata (__mdt). Tooling mode matches ApexClass, FlowDefinition, Flow versions, and other Tooling entities. Results respect your Salesforce permissions.
