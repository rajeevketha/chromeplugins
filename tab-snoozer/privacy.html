Tab Snoozer Privacy

Last updated: August 4, 2026 · Version 1.8.2

Tab Snoozer is a local Chrome extension. It helps you park a tab and open it again later. It is designed to collect as little as possible.

What is stored

When you snooze a tab, Tab Snoozer saves on your device:

  • Page URL
  • Page title
  • Wake time and when you snoozed it
  • Simple settings (auto-open, show page side tab)

This data is stored with chrome.storage.local in your browser profile.

What is not collected

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No cookies or passwords
  • No page content scraping
  • No browsing history API access
  • No analytics, ads, or remote servers
  • Nothing is uploaded from your machine by this extension

Permissions

  • tabs — read the active tab’s URL/title, close it, and reopen it later
  • storage — keep your snoozed list and settings on this device
  • alarms — wake snoozed tabs at the chosen time
  • notifications — remind you when a snoozed tab is due
  • scripting — show the in-page reminder toast on the site you are viewing
  • http(s) host access — on-page Snooze side tab and ready toast only; no page content collection

Limits

  • Only http:// and https:// pages can be snoozed
  • Internal Chrome pages and local files are blocked

Your control

You can open, remove, or clear snoozed tabs from the popup. Removing the extension deletes its local storage.
